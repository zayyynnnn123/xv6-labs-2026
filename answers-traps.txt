1. Which registers contain function arguments? Which holds 13?
The argument registers are a0–a7. The value 13 is in a2, the third argument to printf.

2. Where is the call to f in main? Where is the call to g?
Neither call appears explicitly. The compiler optimized them away: it replaced f(8) + 1 
with 12 and inlined the calculation from g into f.

3. At what address is printf located?
At 0x746, as shown by jal 746 <printf>.

4. What value is in ra just after the jalr to printf in main?
The return address is 0x3c, the address of the instruction immediately after the call to printf.
